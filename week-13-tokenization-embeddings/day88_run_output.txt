1. A WORD AS ITS PIECES -- character n-grams and hashing
======================================================================
Each word is wrapped in < and >, then cut into every piece of 3 to 6 characters. Each piece is hashed (32-bit FNV-1a, as in fastText) into one of 200,000 rows.

'where': 14 n-grams: <wh whe her ere re> <whe wher here ere> <wher where here> <where where>
the piece '<her>' (the whole word 'her') is among the n-grams of 'her': True; of 'where': False ('where' has 'her' but not '<her>')
the 21650 known words have 125,097 distinct n-grams; they fill 93,157 of the 200,000 rows (31,940 n-grams share a row with another); a word has 23.1 n-grams on average
