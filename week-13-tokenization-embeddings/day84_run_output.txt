characters in the test text never seen in training: 4
  U+005E: 1 byte(s) in UTF-8; in 2 test word(s)
  U+00B4: 2 byte(s) in UTF-8; in 1 test word(s)
  U+03C5: 2 byte(s) in UTF-8; in 1 test word(s)
  U+2014: 3 byte(s) in UTF-8; in 2 test word(s)
test words with such a character: 6 -- BPE keeps the character as a symbol of its own, which a model trained on this vocabulary has never seen. A byte-level BPE (as in GPT-2) starts from the 256 possible byte values instead, so no input can ever fall outside it.
