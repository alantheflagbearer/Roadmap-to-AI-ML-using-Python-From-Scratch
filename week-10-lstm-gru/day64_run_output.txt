======================================================================
6. WHAT COMES NEXT
======================================================================
The forward pass is done and its cache holds every gate value at every step. Day 65 walks backward through that cache -- two gradients flowing back in time, one for h and one for c -- and checks the result numerically. After that the LSTM can finally be trained.
