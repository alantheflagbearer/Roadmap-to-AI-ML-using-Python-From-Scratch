3. THE DIRECT ROUTE, AS A GRADIENT
======================================================================
Day 63 measured the cell-state line going forward. Going backward, every step multiplies dL/dc by that step's forget gate. With the gates cut off from h (the rows of W that multiply h set to zero) that line is the ONLY route from c_T back to c_0, so dL/dc_0 must equal dL/dc_T times the product of the forget gates, exactly. 16 units, 200 sequences of 30 N(0,1) inputs, forget bias 2.
