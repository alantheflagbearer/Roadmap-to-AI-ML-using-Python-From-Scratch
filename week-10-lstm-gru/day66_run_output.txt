3. THE DIRECT ROUTE -- the update gate plays the forget gate's part
======================================================================
Going back one step, h = z * h_prev + ... sends dh * z straight to h_prev. With the gates and the candidate cut off from h (W's hidden rows set to zero), that is the only route from h_T back to h_0, so dL/dh_0 must be dL/dh_T times the product of the update gates. 16 units, 200 sequences of 30 N(0,1) inputs, keep-bias 2.
