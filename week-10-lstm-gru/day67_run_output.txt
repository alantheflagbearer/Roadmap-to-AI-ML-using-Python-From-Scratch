======================================================================
8. WHAT COMES NEXT
======================================================================
Every tagger today reads left to right, so the tag of a word is decided before the words after it are seen. Day 62 measured what that costs: a right-to-left tagger is right on many words the left-to-right one gets wrong. Day 68 builds the two standard extensions: stacking cells in layers, and a bidirectional tagger that reads both ways at once.
