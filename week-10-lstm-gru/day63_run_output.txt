4. DAY 59'S QUESTION, ASKED OF THE LSTM -- how much of a nudge at step 0 survives T steps?
======================================================================
16 units, 200 sequences of N(0,1) inputs -- Day 59's setup. The state at step 0 is nudged by a tiny amount in a random direction, and the size of the change at step T is divided by the size of the nudge. 1.0 means fully kept; below 1 fading; above 1 growing. RNN: the hidden state, at five spectral radii of Whh (Day 59's sweep, same weights). LSTM: the cell state, at four forget-gate biases, all other weights random. Nothing is trained.

6. WHAT TODAY DID NOT SHOW
======================================================================
Every gate today was set by hand or left random. Section 3 shows a cell CAN hold a number for 1,000 steps; Section 4 shows that with the forget gate near 1 a nudge survives where the RNN's fades or explodes. Neither shows that training FINDS such gate values. That needs the forward pass over whole sequences (Day 64), the backward pass with a gradient check (Day 65), and a real comparison of trained RNN, LSTM and GRU (Day 67).
