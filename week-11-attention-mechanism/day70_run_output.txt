7. DOES REVERSING HELP? -- Sutskever et al.'s trick at longer lengths
======================================================================
REVERSE instead of COPY, everything else as Section 2 at hidden 32 (40000 steps, three seeds). Reversing puts the first digits to write next to the encoder's end, but the LAST digits to write are then the ones read longest ago.

8. WHAT COMES NEXT
======================================================================
The decoder fails because it may only look at one summary of the input. Day 71 lets it look back at EVERY encoder state, choosing at each output step which ones to read: additive (Bahdanau) attention.
