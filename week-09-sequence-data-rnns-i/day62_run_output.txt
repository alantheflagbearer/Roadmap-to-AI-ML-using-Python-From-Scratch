2. THE ASSEMBLED MODEL -- gradient check through all six parts at once
======================================================================
A tiny tagger (12 words, 3 tags, embedding 4, hidden 5) on four sentences of lengths 5, 2, 4, 1, with one word repeated. Every entry of every parameter is nudged by +-1e-5 and the change in the masked loss is compared with the gradient the assembled backward pass computed. Pass rule: |numeric - analytic| <= 1e-10 + 1e-6 * |numeric| for every entry.

7. A LIMIT -- this RNN reads left to right only
======================================================================
The hidden state at word t has seen words 0..t and nothing after it. To measure what that leaves out, the SAME tagger is trained on every sentence written backwards -- a right-to-left reader, which sees only what FOLLOWS each word -- and its predictions are flipped back and compared, word by word, with the left-to-right tagger's. 8. THE WEEK IN ONE PARAGRAPH
======================================================================
Day 56 showed why sequences need something new; Day 57 built the recurrent cell; Day 58 trained it with BPTT; Day 59 measured how gradient fades or explodes over long distances; Day 60 capped the step so explosions cannot wreck training; Day 61 put real text in. Today those six parts became one model that tags every word of a sentence. What still limits it is what Day 59 measured: a plain RNN cannot carry information far. Week 10 replaces the cell with one built for that: the LSTM (Day 63).
