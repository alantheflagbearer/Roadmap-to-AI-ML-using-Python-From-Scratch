1. BY HAND -- what a detector's accuracy means when fakes are rare
======================================================================
a detector that catches 95% of generated images (true positive rate) and wrongly flags 5% of real ones (false positive rate);
share of flagged images that really are generated = 0.95 p / (0.95 p + 0.05 (1 - p)), p = the share of images that are generated
       p  flagged that are generated  real images flagged per generated one caught
   0.500                       0.950                                          0.05
   0.100                       0.679                                          0.47
   0.010                       0.161                                          5.21
   0.001                       0.019                                         52.58
by hand for p = 0.01: 0.95 x 0.01 = 0.0095; 0.05 x 0.99 = 0.0495; 0.0095 / (0.0095 + 0.0495) = 0.161
