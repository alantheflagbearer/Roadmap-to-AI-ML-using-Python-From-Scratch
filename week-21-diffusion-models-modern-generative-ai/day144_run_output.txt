1. BY HAND -- guidance in the x_0 guess is guidance in the noise guess
======================================================================
eps(x_0) = (x_t - sqrt(abar_t) x_0) / sqrt(1 - abar_t) is a straight-line function of x_0, so mixing guesses commutes with it:
  w = 0.0: largest difference between eps(guided x_0) and guided eps: 0.00e+00
  w = 1.0: largest difference between eps(guided x_0) and guided eps: 4.44e-16
  w = 3.0: largest difference between eps(guided x_0) and guided eps: 1.78e-15
w = 0 returns the no-caption guess exactly, w = 1 the captioned guess exactly; a negative prompt simply takes the no-caption guess's place
